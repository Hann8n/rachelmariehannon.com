I Might Be An Idiot

Based on True Events

A memoir by Rachel Marie Hannon

About I Might Be An Idiot

I Might Be An Idiot follows my determined search for popularity as I maintain trails in Arizona following a suspension from my private arts Pennsylvania college.

When things get worse it forces me to search for the reason why all these things kept happening to me. In the end… well, you read the title.

Comps

  • Wild by Cheryl Strayed – Memoir of a woman who resolves conflict with a series of flashbacks.
